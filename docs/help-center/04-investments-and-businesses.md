# Investments and businesses

## Investments

Open **Investments** (`/investments`) to manage investment holdings and investment events. Holdings represent positions associated with investment accounts. Investment events record activity that changes or explains a position.

Keep quantities, cost information, dates, currencies and event types accurate. Events include buys, sells, dividends, interest, contributions and withdrawals. Interest and dividend events are linked to a holding and its account; they record income and update that account balance. Open holding values are included separately from the linked investment account's remaining cash balance in consolidated net-worth snapshots. Investment values and performance views depend on the records and valuation information available in TrellisFlow. Do not interpret unrealized gains as realized cash profit.

TrellisFlow's product help is informational and does not provide personalized investment, tax or legal advice.

## Businesses

Open **Businesses** (`/businesses`) to manage business containers and their financial records. Business records are separate from personal records so users can understand activity at the appropriate level.

Business net worth, profit and loss, and cash-runway metrics keep each account currency separate; TrellisFlow does not convert or add unlike currencies. Internal-to-external conversion is blocked when account currencies differ from the business's configured currency, so balances are not silently combined into a single-currency summary. Business reports depend on transactions and other information recorded for the selected business. External business information may be manually maintained or synchronized when a supported integration is configured.

## Keeping records useful

Use consistent categories, descriptions, dates and currencies. Review imported or synchronized records before relying on reports.
