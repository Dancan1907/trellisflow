# Investments and businesses

## Investments

Open **Investments** (`/investments`) to manage investment holdings and investment events. Holdings represent positions associated with investment accounts. Investment events record activity that changes or explains a position.

Keep quantities, cost information, dates, currencies and event types accurate. Investment values and performance views depend on the records and valuation information available in TrellisFlow. Do not interpret unrealized gains as realized cash profit.

TrellisFlow's product help is informational and does not provide personalized investment, tax or legal advice.

## Businesses

Open **Businesses** (`/businesses`) to manage business containers and their financial records. Business records are separate from personal records so users can understand activity at the appropriate level.

Business reports depend on transactions and other information recorded for the selected business. External business information may be manually maintained or synchronized when a supported integration is configured.

## Keeping records useful

Use consistent categories, descriptions, dates and currencies. Review imported or synchronized records before relying on reports.
