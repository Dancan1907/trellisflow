# Budgets, savings goals and debt

## Budgets

Budgets let you set a spending target for a category and monitor activity against it. Open **Budgets** (`/budgets`) to review existing budgets or create one.

Choose the relevant container and category, enter the budget amount and currency, and select the period. Available periods include daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly and custom, subject to the form's supported options.

A budget is a planning tool. Actual spending depends on the transactions recorded and categorized for the relevant period. Incorrect or uncategorized transactions can affect how useful the budget view is.

## Savings goals

Open **Goals** (`/goals`) to create a target and track progress. A goal can include a target amount, currency and optional target date. Contributions record progress toward the goal.

A goal is a tracking and planning feature. It does not move money between accounts automatically unless a separate, explicitly confirmed workflow is provided.

## Debt payoff plans

Open **Debt** (`/debts`) to manage a payoff plan for an eligible debt account. TrellisFlow supports payoff strategies such as snowball and avalanche, along with configured payment assumptions.

Projected payoff dates and interest figures depend on the plan inputs. They are estimates, not lender statements or guarantees. Confirm actual interest rates, minimum payments and balances with the lender.
