# 01 — Vision

## The One-Sentence Vision

> **TrellisFlow is a personal wealth platform where anyone — employee, freelancer, or business owner — can track their entire financial world in one place. It shows Net Worth as the headline and Cash Flow as the engine driving it, and it handles both personal money and business money, whether that business lives inside TrellisFlow or on its own separate system.**

## Why This Vision

Most personal finance apps assume a user is one thing:

- A budgeter (Mint, YNAB).
- An investor (Personal Capital, Sharesight).
- A business owner (QuickBooks, Wave).

Real people are combinations of all three. A single person might:

- Be employed at a company.
- Own stocks and crypto.
- Run a freelance practice.
- Own a small shop.
- Have equity in a larger business.

TrellisFlow is the tool that fits **this** person — not a fictional single-role user.

## The Core Distinction

TrellisFlow makes a design decision that no mainstream personal finance app makes:

> **A user's businesses may exist outside TrellisFlow, and TrellisFlow still includes them in net worth.**

This is the "holding-company view for ordinary people." A user with a sugarcane farm that has its own operational site doesn't have to abandon TrellisFlow or build custom integrations. The farm pushes a _summary_ to TrellisFlow; TrellisFlow includes it in net worth.

This is what makes TrellisFlow genuinely different from "yet another Mint clone."

## Product Shape

- **Headline:** Total Net Worth — the scoreboard.
- **Engine:** Cash Flow — the driver.
- **Containers:** Personal, Investments, Internal Businesses, External Businesses — the four parts of every financial world.
- **Unified:** All four containers roll up into one number.
- **Optional:** A user with only Personal money uses TrellisFlow like a normal finance app. A user with businesses uses it like a holding company dashboard. Neither is second-class.

## The Name

- **Trellis** — the lattice that supports growing plants. A structure that enables growth.
- **Flow** — money moving through the system.

Together: a structure that supports money movement. The name is intentional.

## What TrellisFlow Is Not

- **Not a bank.** TrellisFlow doesn't hold money. It tracks money that lives elsewhere.
- **Not an accounting system.** It tracks money, not tax filings.
- **Not a trading platform.** It records investment positions; it doesn't execute trades.
- **Not a social product.** No sharing, no followers, no collaboration.
- **Not enterprise software.** It's for individuals; businesses are one type of container, not the point.

## Long-Term Vision

TrellisFlow could eventually:

- Connect to bank and brokerage APIs for automatic sync.
- Offer a public API for third-party tools.
- Support family/household accounts (with a redesign, not retrofitting).
- Provide a mobile app (the REST API is already designed for it).
- Integrate with external business platforms via webhooks.

None of these are in the MVP. The architecture is designed so they can be added without rewriting the core.
